Item 1.

Business

Acme designs and sells rocket-powered roller skates to customers around the world. The company sells through its online store and through third-party retailers in many countries.

Acme has three reportable segments: Americas, Europe, and Asia Pacific. Each segment is managed separately and serves customers in its region with the full product line.

12

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company depends on a single supplier in one country for rocket motors, and any disruption to that supplier's operations could materially harm the Company's supply chain and results of operations. Natural disasters, trade restrictions and tariffs could all interrupt deliveries for extended periods of time.

Competition in the roller skate market is intense, and competitors may introduce cheaper products that reduce demand for the Company's products and lower its gross margin over time.
